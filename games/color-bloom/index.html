How to play Color Bloom

  1. Pick a picture, then pick a level. Easy has big pieces, Expert has tiny ones.
  2. Tap a numbered color at the bottom. Every spot with that number turns gray.
  3. Tap or drag over the gray spots to paint them. Pinch or scroll to zoom in.
  4. Stuck? The light bulb shows you a spot you missed.
