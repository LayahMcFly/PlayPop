How to play

  1. 1Draw a line from a plane to the building it needs. The badges above it show the order. Then draw it to the take-off runway.
  2. 2Keep lines on the grey ground. Planes can't cross the grass.
  3. 3Tap a moving plane to pause it. Tap again to go.
  4. 4Every plane you send off earns an upgrade. Tap the glowing arrow on a building to speed it up.
  5. 5Planes that crash or wait too long cost you a heart.
