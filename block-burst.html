Block Burst

Drop blocks, fill rows and columns, and burst them away!

How to play

Drag a piece from the tray onto the board
Fill a whole row or column to clear it
Clear lines back to back for combo bonuses
Clear blocks with a star to collect coins
Stuck? Use dynamite, a bomb or a refresh. No room left for any piece ends the game
