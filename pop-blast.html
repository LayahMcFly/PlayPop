Pop Blast

Aim, shoot and match 3 or more bubbles of the same colour to clear the board.

How to play

Drag to aim, then let go to shoot. Drag back down to cancel.
Match 3 or more of the same colour to pop them. Bubbles left hanging will drop.
Tap the bubbles by the cannon to swap them.
Clear the board before your shots run out, and keep bubbles above the red line.
