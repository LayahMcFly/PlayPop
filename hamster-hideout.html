Hamster Hideout

A hamster is hiding in every colour. Can you find where they all are?

How to play

Hide exactly one hamster in every colour.
Only one hamster in each row and each column.
Hamsters can't touch, not even corner to corner.
Tap a cell to mark it X. Tap again for a hamster.
A wrong hamster costs a seed. Lose all 3 and you try again.
